About Bula Air

Fiji hospitality, modern travel.

Bula Air is a fictional Fiji-based airline designed around clear booking, relaxed premium service, and a visual identity inspired by a deep-blue fuselage, sky-blue tail, turquoise wave, and hibiscus detail.

Our route network centers on Nadi, connecting island travelers to Auckland, Sydney, Los Angeles, and nearby Pacific destinations.

Hubs

  • Nadi (NAN), main hub
  • Auckland (AKL), regional gateway
  • Sydney (SYD), trans-Tasman partner city
